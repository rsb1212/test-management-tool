## Slide 2: The Administrative Dashboard
*(00:06 – 00:13)*

Upon successful login, we land on the central Dashboard — the command center of NexureAI.

Notice the personalized welcome header, which immediately establishes context. The left navigation panel provides structured access to all core modules: Dashboard, Roles & Permissions, User Management, Work Orders, Audit Logs, and Reports.

At the top, we see the user's current context — marked as "Administrator" — with the ability to switch administrative profiles via the dropdown. The Quick Actions bar offers one-click access to high-frequency tasks: Manage Users, Configuration, and Export Reports.

The real-time metrics cards give us an instant health check of the system:
- **Active Work Orders** and **Pending Approvals** both at zero, indicating a clean operational state.

- **Total Users** at 54, showing platform adoption scale.

Below that, the Department SLA Composition chart provides visual analytics for drilling down into work order distribution across departments.

## Slide 3: Roles & Permissions Management
*(00:13 – 00:20)*

Next, let's explore the governance backbone of the platform — the Roles & Permissions module.

Under the "Roles" tab, we see a comprehensive table outlining every defined role within the organization. Each row provides:
- **Role Name** — the identity of the role,
- **Description** — its business purpose,
- **User Count** — how many users are assigned,
- **Access Level** and **Permissions** — visual indicators of privilege scope,
- **Created Date** — for audit trail purposes.

Currently, the system defines five distinct roles: Business Reviewer, UAT Manager, UAT User, Admin, and Super Admin. Notably, the UAT Manager role has 19 assigned users, while Super Admin — the highest privilege tier — has 35 users. This granular visibility allows administrators to quickly identify role distribution and detect any access anomalies.

---

## Slide 4: The Permission Matrix
*(00:20 – 00:29)*

Now, let's switch to the Permission Matrix tab — this is where fine-grained access control happens.

This matrix provides a cross-tabulated view of every system permission against every organizational role. Permissions are categorized by functional domain — you can see tags like "Permission," "Product," "Role," and "Testcase" — making it easy to navigate complex entitlement structures at scale.

Each intersection shows whether a specific role holds a particular permission. The "×" marks indicate restricted access, while granted permissions would be clearly marked. This visual matrix eliminates the guesswork from access reviews and simplifies compliance audits. Administrators can instantly verify who can create or delete products, manage roles, approve test cases, or modify system configurations.

With the Refresh button, the matrix stays synchronized with real-time permission changes across the platform.

That concludes our walkthrough of NexureAI. From secure authentication to real-time operational dashboards and granular role-based access control, the platform is built to give administrators confidence, visibility, and control — every single release.
